Projects

Badge Database

Every Twitch badge, and exactly when to get it. A live database of Twitch global badges with countdowns and a schedule of every badge event, an X bot that announces new badges the moment they appear, and a 24/7 Twitch stream with 1,800+ followers.

Role
Design, code, operations
Year
2026
Status
Live
01 · Timeline

Every badge event on one schedule

Running and upcoming badge events with live countdowns, split into watch and sub rows, filterable by free or paid. Yesterday is always the first column, so nothing that just ended goes missing.

02 · Badge pages

A page for every badge

The objective, category, start and end, a live countdown, a preview of the badge next to your own name in chat, and one click to add it to your calendar.

03 · Archive

The full archive, searchable

All 450+ global badge sets, sorted by date added, user count or title, with filters for free, paid, active, ending soon and upcoming. Each one is a pre-rendered page, so search engines find them too.

04 · X bot

Announced the moment it appears

A bot posts every new Twitch badge to X with a generated image within minutes, and follows up with "now available" and "ending soon" posts for badge events. 150+ badges announced so far.

05 · 24/7 stream

A stream that never sleeps

An overlay I built for the round-the-clock Twitch stream: a live clock, the badges you can earn in the current category, and every event with its countdown. 1,800+ followers.

06 · Reminders

Reminders that come to you

Visitors can ask for a browser notification when dates are announced, when a badge starts and before it ends, sent by my own push server. No account needed.
