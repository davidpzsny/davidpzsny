2. The short version

  • This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads or tracking.
  • I only receive personal data when you write to me, through the contact form or by email.
  • I use it only to reply to you and, if we work together, for the project.
  • I never sell your data or use it for marketing.

3. When you write to me

What I receive

Your name, your email address, the type of project you picked, your message, and anything else you choose to include.

Why

To answer your message, discuss your project and, if you ask for one, prepare a quote.

Legal basis

Taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). For messages that are not about a project, my legitimate interest in replying to people who contact me (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How long

As long as needed to handle your enquiry. If no project follows, I delete the conversation within 12 months of the last message. If we work together, I keep what the project needs for as long as the project and the law require, for example accounting rules for invoices.

Please don't send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information through the form.

4. Service providers

A few trusted services help run this site. They only process data as needed to provide their service:

  • Formspree, Inc. (United States) delivers the messages sent through the contact form to my inbox. Privacy policy
  • Google (Gmail) is the mailbox where messages arrive. Privacy policy
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States) hosts this website through GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it may log technical data such as your IP address for security and reliability. Privacy statement
  • Twitch Interactive, Inc. (United States): the badge images in the project examples load from Twitch's image servers, so your browser connects to Twitch, which can see your IP address and browser details. Privacy notice

The fonts are hosted on this site itself, so no font service sees your visit.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Some of the providers above are based in the United States. Transfers to them rely on the safeguards described in their own policies, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies, does not store anything in your browser, and does not use analytics or advertising tools. That is also why there is no cookie banner.
